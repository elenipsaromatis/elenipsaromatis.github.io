About me

I spent four years in wet lab environments, where I specialized in synthetic organic chemistry, making berberine derivatives and analyzing them with chromatographic and spectroscopic methods. That work made me want faster ways to decide which molecules are worth making.

So I learned to code. In 2026 I finished an MSc in Digital Chemistry with AI and Automation at Imperial College London with a Distinction. For my thesis in the OptiML PSE group, I built a Pareto-front-guided search over the latent space of a SMILES VAE to optimize several molecular properties at once.

I’ve also programmed Opentrons robots to run automated experiments with a spectrophotometer. I want to work where AI meets chemistry in pharmaceutical R&D.
